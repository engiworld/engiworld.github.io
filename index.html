Overview

Autonomous agents have made rapid progress in general-purpose computer use, yet reliable automation of professional industrial engineering remains out of reach. Engineering workflows require reasoning over geometric and physical constraints and keeping dependencies consistent across software and design stages.

We present EngiWorld, a benchmark designed around the capabilities that real engineering projects require, from operating a single application to solving engineering problems end to end. It contains 1,301 expert-curated tasks spanning six engineering domains (CAD, CAE, CAM, BIM, EDA, and DCC) and 26 professional software applications, with both GUI and CLI interfaces. The tasks are organized into six types, corresponding to capabilities that real engineering projects require.

We further introduce an artifact-centric evaluation methodology built on a unified suite of domain verifiers. The verifiers programmatically check whether final and intermediate artifacts are geometrically valid, physically feasible, and compliant with design rules. Beyond binary success, they also score how well optimized designs meet their specifications. Evaluation of seven frontier models reveals a substantial capability gap: the strongest model scores only 44.7/100, and just 3.6% of runs on tasks that require cross-software coordination succeed. EngiWorld provides a rigorous foundation for measuring progress toward agents that operate professional engineering software end to end.

The benchmark

Six task types, testing the capabilities real engineering projects require.

Single-Software Execution

931

Model, modify, or simulate an artifact in one designated application.

Software Selection

140

Choose suitable software from a permitted set and complete the task.

Vision-Guided Modeling

120

Build engineering models from reference drawings and visual requirements.

Design Optimization

40

Optimize design quality while satisfying engineering feasibility constraints.

Cross-Software Coordination

60

Carry out workflows that pass dependent artifacts across applications.

Open-Environment Engineering

10

Choose and install software in a blank environment, then solve the engineering problem end to end.

How the dataset is constructed

Experts define tasks and acceptance criteria; AI assists specification and verifier implementation. Each task is executed and reviewed in its software environment.

Evaluation

Agents work through GUI or CLI interfaces. Domain-specific verifiers reopen their deliverables and check the engineering properties that matter.

Interact

Start from a clean, task-specific workspace. Use visual observations and mouse or keyboard actions, or terminal commands and scripts.

Deliver

Submit native models, simulation outputs, circuits, building models, or 3D scenes, including intermediate files when the workflow requires them.

Verify

Recompute geometry, topology, physical quantities, rule compliance, and consistency across workflow stages against task-specific criteria.

Analysis

Controlled ablations vary visual inputs, accessibility information, screenshot resolution, and interaction history.

Reference images in the message

For GPT-5.6 Sol, GUI EngiScore rises from 15.0 to 31.7 when the reference drawing is shown directly in the task message. Easier access helps agents find information but does not ensure that they use it correctly.

Richer observations have tradeoffs

Accessibility trees improve Kimi, barely change GPT, and lower Gemini’s score. API cost rises by 51–148% even though all three models use fewer turns.

History length is model dependent

Gemini benefits from a 15-turn window; GPT largely plateaus after 10 turns. Kimi performs best at 10, with a longer window reducing its score and increasing cost.

Case studies

Four Single-Software Execution GUI workflows from the paper. Each passes its task-specific artifact checks and receives a score of 1.0.

EDA / KiCad

Connect a data bus and preserve the circuit

Eight distinct data nets establish the required MCU–SRAM mapping. The saved schematic retains the existing circuitry and passes with zero electrical-rule errors.

CAD / AutoCAD

Build a precise staggered hole pattern

Two rectangular arrays create 23 tube holes. The exported DXF preserves the required coordinates, radii, entity types, and layer assignments.

CAD / SolidWorks

Align parts while preserving geometry

The sliding block is positioned against the base. The exported STEP retains both independent solids and their original dimensions.

DCC / Blender

Deliver a scene with a packed AO bake

A 512 × 512 ambient-occlusion image is baked and packed into the saved native scene, preserving the computed pixels in the deliverable.

Where Cross-Software Coordination workflows break down

The recorded failures span import-scale errors, incorrect action targeting, and mismatches between submitted representations and verifier expectations. In the load case, a pressure representation does not satisfy the verifier’s force/displacement check; in the CSV case, an unrecognized diameter-field name stops evaluation before downstream geometry checks.
